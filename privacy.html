ReplyPilot

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 May 2026

Overview

ReplyPilot helps small businesses manage WhatsApp customer conversations, FAQ replies, lead capture, appointment scheduling, and calendar availability checks. This policy explains what information the service handles and how it is used.

Information We Process

ReplyPilot may process business profile details, FAQ content, appointment labels, customer names, phone numbers, WhatsApp message text, booking requests, conversation history, and calendar availability or event details needed to confirm appointments.

How We Use Information

Information is used to generate replies, answer FAQs, detect leads, check whether a requested appointment slot is available, create confirmed calendar bookings, preserve conversation context for the same customer number, and show the business a dashboard of recent conversations and setup status.

Google Calendar Data

When a business connects Google Calendar, ReplyPilot uses calendar access only to read free or busy availability and create appointment events that the business has asked the service to manage. ReplyPilot does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to unrelated third parties.

Data Sharing

ReplyPilot shares data only with service providers needed to operate the product, such as hosting, database, AI response, WhatsApp delivery, and calendar integration providers. Data is not sold.

Data Retention

Conversation, lead, and business configuration records are kept while the business uses ReplyPilot. A business can request deletion of its configuration and conversation records.

Security

ReplyPilot uses hosted infrastructure and environment-managed secrets for production credentials. Access tokens and API keys should not be stored in source control.

Contact

For privacy requests or questions, contact the ReplyPilot operator through the support email configured in the Google Cloud OAuth consent screen.
