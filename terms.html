Use of ReplyPilot

ReplyPilot is provided to help businesses automate customer replies, capture leads, and manage appointment scheduling workflows. Businesses are responsible for reviewing replies and ensuring their customer communications are accurate and lawful.

Connected Services

ReplyPilot may connect to WhatsApp, Google Calendar, AI providers, hosting providers, and database services to perform the requested automation. Access should be granted only by an authorized business operator.

Limitations

ReplyPilot is an automation aid and does not replace human judgment. Businesses should monitor conversations, booking accuracy, and escalation workflows.

Contact

For service questions, contact the ReplyPilot operator through the support email configured in the Google Cloud OAuth consent screen.
